# Install Policy Lookup

Download the installer for your computer from the **Releases** page
(right side of the GitHub page → *Releases* → latest version).

## Windows

1. Download **`PolicyLookup-Setup-Windows.exe`** and open it.
2. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Click **Next → Install → Finish**.
4. Policy Lookup opens. Next time, open it from the **Start menu** or the **desktop icon**.

## Mac (Apple Silicon — M1 or newer)

1. Download **`PolicyLookup-Mac.zip`** and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **Policy Lookup** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Policy Lookup** from Applications.
   The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then open
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Policy Lookup opens. Next time, open it like any other app.

## First use

- **Add documents:** drag PDFs, scans or Word files into the left panel.
- **Ask a question** in plain English or Hinglish.
- **Optional, once:** click **Download local AI model** (about 2 GB, needs internet one
  time) for short AI answers. Search works without it.
- **Close:** click **Quit Policy Lookup** in the left panel.

Your documents stay on your computer. The app works with the internet switched off.
